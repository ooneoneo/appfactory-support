Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-20.

Apps covered

APP_FACTORY is a collection of standalone utility apps for iOS and Android, developed and distributed by Arment Asllani under the APP_FACTORY brand. This policy currently covers the following apps (the “Apps”):

  • A01 Shift & Pay (Skift & Lön)
  • A02 Receipt & Warranty (Kvitto & Garanti)
  • A03 Subscriptions — Cost Tracker (Abonnemang)
  • A04 Interval Timer — Training (Intervalltimer)
  • A05 Packing List — Travel (Packlista)
  • A06 Row Counter — Knit & Crochet (Varvräknare)
  • A07 Parking — Find My Car (Parkering)

Local-first

All Apps are local-first. Your data (shifts, receipts, subscriptions, workouts, lists, projects, parking spots) is stored only on your own device via local SQLite. No login is required and no user data is sent to our servers.

Data we collect

We have no backend. Data the Apps handle themselves:

Reminders/APNs token A01, A03 Only if you explicitly turn on reminders. Can be turned off in the app. No

We do NOT collect

  • Name, email, phone number (unless you voluntarily contact us via support).
  • Advertising ID / tracking ID (we use no advertising SDKs).
  • Browsing history, search history.
  • Diagnostic data (we send no crash reports or performance data).
  • Health or fitness data (stored only locally on your device).
  • Contacts, user-generated content outside the app.

Third parties

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — handles purchases and restoration.
  • RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) — handles entitlement validation for Pro features.
  • Expo / EAS — builds and distributes the apps.
  • GitHub Pages — serves this website.

These third parties have their own privacy policies; we recommend you read them.

Children

The Apps are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Since all data is local, you can delete the app at any time (this removes all local data). For Pro purchases: use “Restore Purchases” in the app (RevenueCat) or App Store → Settings → Purchases.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
